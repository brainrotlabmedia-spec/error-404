Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is a system of biological processes by which photosynthetic organisms, such as most plants, algae, and cyanobacteria, convert light energy, typically from sunlight, into the chemical energy necessary to fuel their metabolisms.

Process

In plants, photosynthesis takes place in chloroplasts, which contain the chlorophyll. Photosynthesis is largely responsible for producing and maintaining the oxygen content of the Earth's atmosphere.
